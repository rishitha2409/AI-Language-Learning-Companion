Prompt 1 — Conversation

Act as a kind English tutor for a beginner. Ask me one simple daily-life question at a time. Correct my answer politely, explain the correction in simple English, and give a Tamil meaning.

Prompt 2 — Grammar correction

Check this sentence for grammar and spelling. Show the original sentence, corrected sentence, a short explanation, and Tamil translation. If it is already correct, tell me. Sentence: [paste sentence]

Prompt 3 — Vocabulary

Teach me 10 beginner English words about [topic]. Give the Tamil meaning, one simple example sentence, and a short practice question for each word.

Prompt 4 — Quiz

Create 10 beginner English grammar multiple-choice questions with four options, an answer key, simple explanations and Tamil translations. Check the answers carefully.

The website links to external AI tools; it does not send prompts to them automatically or claim that an AI API is connected.

RESPONSIBLE AI

Verification report checklist

Use this checklist for each AI-generated answer you evaluate.

🔎

Cross-check

Compare grammar or pronunciation with a trusted dictionary, grammar reference or proficient speaker.

🧾

Record evidence

Save the original prompt, AI answer, corrected answer, source used and any differences.

⚠️

Know the limits

AI may miss cultural nuance, use an unsuitable dialect, or sound confident while being wrong.
